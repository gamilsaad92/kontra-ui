---
name: Kontra strategic direction
description: Product boundary from the developer brief: own pre-settlement transaction operations, not tokenization or settlement infrastructure.
---

Kontra is the AI transaction workspace and operating system for private transactions. Its core responsibility ends at verified, structured, auditable closing data. Tokenization is an optional downstream outcome and should be supported through portable metadata, exports, and integration adapters rather than issuance, wallet, custody, or settlement infrastructure.

**Why:** The strategic developer brief explicitly says to build everything before settlement and integrate with future tokenization providers instead of competing with them.

**How to apply:** Treat workflow packs, document intelligence, role collaboration, approvals, audit trails, transaction analytics, and verified closing packages as core. Keep digital-asset preparation optional and additive; avoid expanding blockchain, exchange, marketplace, wallet, custody, stablecoin, or settlement features as first-class Kontra product surfaces.
