## The main issue

For a room identified as a **Marriott Hotel Acquisition**, the record currently asks for broad fields such as:

* Primary party
* Counterparty
* Deal value
* Key financial terms
* Governing documents
* Governing jurisdiction

Those fields are technically universal, but they do not demonstrate that Kontra truly understands the transaction.

A buyer opening this room should see hotel-acquisition fields such as:

### Transaction

* Purchase price
* Earnest money deposit
* Due-diligence expiration
* Financing contingency
* Target closing date
* Current stage

### Asset / Company

* Property name
* Property address
* Hotel brand or flag
* Property type
* Number of rooms or keys
* Current owner entity
* Property manager
* Franchise or management agreement
* Parcel or title reference

### Parties

* Buyer
* Seller
* Buyer’s representative
* Seller’s representative
* Legal counsel
* Lender
* Title or escrow company
* Property inspector

### Ownership

* Current titled owner
* Proposed acquiring entity
* Beneficial owners
* Ownership percentages
* Existing liens or encumbrances

### Financial

* Purchase price
* Historical revenue
* NOI
* Occupancy
* ADR
* RevPAR
* Existing debt
* Proposed financing
* Required equity
* Capital-expenditure obligations

### Legal

* Purchase agreement
* Title status
* Survey status

* Franchise agreement

### Approvals

* Buyer approval

Kontra does not need to show every field immediately. But the schema should clearly reflect the selected workflow pack.

## What to tell Replit

> The universal Transaction Record categories are correct, but the current fields are too generic. Make the field schema workflow-pack-driven.
>
> Each workflow pack must define:
>
> * `field_key`
> * `display_label`
> * `category`
> * `data_type`
> * `required_or_optional`
> * `applicable_stages`
> * `expected_source_documents`
> * `confirmation_role`
> * `display_order`
>
> Keep the seven universal categories, but load transaction-specific fields inside them.
>
> For the Commercial Real Estate Acquisition pack, include fields specific to the asset and acquisition, such as property address, asset type, buyer, seller, purchase price, title owner, due-diligence deadline, financing, title status, environmental status, and closing approvals.
>
> For hotel acquisitions, allow the generated custom schema to add hotel-specific fields such as brand, room count, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, franchise agreement, management agreement, and brand-transfer approval.
>
> Do not hardcode Marriott or hotels into the core application. Generate these fields from the workflow pack and the original transaction description.
>
> Add two display modes:
>
> **Summary View** — show only populated, required, conflicting, or action-needed fields.
>
> **Full Record** — show all expected fields, including “Not provided.”
>
> Default to Summary View after documents begin arriving so the page does not become an excessively long form.
>
> When a field is missing, provide a contextual action where possible:
>
> * `Upload source document`
> * `Request from participant`
> * `Enter manually`
> * `Mark not applicable`
>
> Do not add new regulated functionality. This remains a coordination, extraction, confirmation, and preparation system.

## One UI recommendation

Right now, every category is expanded and every missing field is visible. That makes the room look incomplete rather than intelligent.

I would default to:

* Transaction: expanded
* Asset / Company: expanded
* Other categories: collapsed
* Categories with conflicts or required missing information: automatically expanded

Each collapsed category should summarize its state:

> **Parties** — 0 of 6 identified
> **Ownership** — 2 items awaiting review
> **Financial** — 1 conflict
> **Legal** — 4 required fields missing
> **Approvals** — Not started

That would make the record feel like an operational system instead of a long questionnaire.

The architecture is right now. The next step is to make the Transaction Record **specific, adaptive, and actionable**, rather than merely comprehensive.
