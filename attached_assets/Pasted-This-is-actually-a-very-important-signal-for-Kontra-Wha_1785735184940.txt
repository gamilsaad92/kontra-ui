If Kontra succeeds, the user shouldn’t have to think:

The transaction should naturally progress from creation to completion inside Kontra, with specialized providers handling payments, custody, tokenization, or settlement through integrations behind the scenes.

One recommendation

I would not build stablecoin payments now.

Instead, tell your developer to add a Settlement Provider abstraction into the architecture.

For example:

SettlementProvider
WireProvider
EscrowProvider
StripeProvider
BridgeProvider
StablecoinProvider
FutureCBDCProvider

Initially, only traditional wire and escrow might be active. The others can remain disabled behind feature flags.

That small architectural decision means that when Stripe, Ramp, Bridge, or another provider becomes relevant for your customers, you plug them in without redesigning the platform. It keeps Kontra focused on its core value—coordinating transactions—while making it ready for how financial infrastructure is evolving.
