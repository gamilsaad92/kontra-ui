# Final Recommendation to the Developer

**No major redesign is needed.**

The product has crossed into the category of a polished enterprise SaaS website. At this point, improvements should focus on polish and storytelling rather than adding more sections or features.

### Remaining priorities (in order):

1. Move the product preview higher on the homepage.
2. Strengthen the hero supporting copy to emphasize transaction outcomes.
3. Make the workspace preview feel alive with subtle animations.
4. Add an "Enterprise Ready" trust section.
5. Make the "Build Your Own" workflow option more prominent since it represents Kontra's core differentiator.
6. Refine tokenization messaging to emphasize producing **tokenization-ready outputs**, not performing issuance.
7. Add a brief guided introduction before users enter the live demo.
