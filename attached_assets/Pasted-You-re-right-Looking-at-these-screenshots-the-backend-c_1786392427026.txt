You're right. Looking at these screenshots, **the backend changed much more than the actual room experience did**.

What Replit built is mostly infrastructure. The room still visually feels like the same Kontra: Overview, Documents, People, the existing Digital Asset Readiness panel, and the same early-stage workflow. The new Settlement system is essentially invisible because this room is at **Stage 1 of 5**, and `SettlementReadinessPanel` only appears when the transaction reaches Settlement/Funded/Complete.

There are also two things in these screenshots that concern me.

First, **People still looks wrong**. Your screenshot shows “Deal Coordinator — Not invited yet,” a `Not invited` badge, and an Invite button. That is precisely what #248 was supposed to remove. So either the frontend fix wasn't deployed to the Vercel instance you're viewing, or this workflow pack uses a coordinator role that wasn't included in their owner-role logic.

Second, the **Digital Asset Readiness** section still doesn't communicate the architecture we just built. It says “Building from your transaction activity,” with Identity & Parties, Asset/Company, Transaction Terms, etc. That's useful, but a user has no indication that Kontra can now take a transaction all the way through settlement and create a Transaction Seal.

So I wouldn't build anything else. **I would make the architecture we already built visible.**

### What I think the room should show

At the top of Overview, underneath Transaction Snapshot, I'd introduce one compact lifecycle:

**Review → Approval → Closing → Settlement → Complete**

The current stage gets highlighted. Future stages remain visible but muted.

Then the Overview becomes progressive rather than dumping future functionality into Stage 1.

At the beginning:

> **Transaction Readiness**
>
> 18% ready
> Building the verified transaction record
>
> ✓ Workspace created
> ○ Parties
> ○ Core documents
> ○ Transaction terms
> ○ Approvals
> ○ Closing
> ○ Settlement

As documents come in, that same area becomes more intelligent.

Near Closing:

> **Closing Readiness — 84%**
>
> 7 of 9 conditions satisfied
> 2 items need attention

And eventually:

> **Settlement Readiness — 89%**
>
> Settlement: Traditional
> 8 of 9 conditions verified
> ⚠ Funding confirmation pending

Then:

> **Transaction Complete ✓**
>
> Transaction Seal
> Completed Aug 19, 2026
> 9/9 conditions verified
>
> View Transaction Seal

That creates **one story from beginning to end** instead of having separate pieces suddenly appear depending on the stage.

### I would also reconsider the label “Digital Asset Readiness”

This screenshot reinforces something I've been thinking.

For this Marriott acquisition, you're showing **Digital Asset Readiness** prominently even though this may be a completely ordinary acquisition.

That makes Kontra look more like a tokenization product than the universal transaction platform we've been designing.

I'd rename the default panel:

**TRANSACTION READINESS**

> **Building the verified transaction record**

Then, if tokenization is enabled, introduce a secondary capability:

**Digital Asset Readiness**
42% ready

So:

**Transaction Readiness = universal**

**Digital Asset Readiness = optional**

**Settlement Readiness = later lifecycle**

That hierarchy is much clearer.
