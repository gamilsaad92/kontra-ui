Participant Room Architecture Fix — Do not redesign the application
We have identified two production issues while testing the new transaction architecture.
1. Deal Coordinator document uploads
The Deal Coordinator/room owner must be allowed to upload any transaction document, including documents assigned to another responsible party.
Role assignment represents responsibility for supplying the document; it must not prevent the Deal Coordinator from uploading a document they already possess.
Example:
LOI responsible party = Buyer
Deal Coordinator uploads LOI
Upload must succeed
Requirement becomes satisfied
Preserve uploaded_by / actor provenance as Deal Coordinator
Do not incorrectly attribute the upload to Buyer
Participants should continue to be limited according to their permissions. This change is specifically to ensure the workspace coordinator can administer the complete transaction.
2. Participant invitation opens legacy room
The invited Buyer is currently routed into what appears to be the legacy participant-room interface containing Evening Standup, Activity Timeline, Your Documents, Transaction Progress, Party Status, and Signal Ready.
This should not remain as a separate legacy experience.
Participants should enter the same current transaction workspace architecture as the Deal Coordinator:
Overview | Documents | People
The experience should then be filtered according to role and permissions rather than rendering an entirely different application.
For example, Buyer Overview should show the same transaction identity/lifecycle and current architecture, but prioritize:
What Kontra needs from the Buyer
Buyer's assigned actions
Documents Buyer is responsible for
Transaction status relevant to Buyer
Information the Buyer has permission to see
Do not expose Deal Coordinator-only controls.
Reuse the current Transaction Record, lifecycle, Documents, actions, provenance, and agent-state architecture rather than maintaining parallel legacy participant components.
3. Fix invitation-entry crash
Opening the Buyer invitation initially produces Minified React error #310. Reloading then opens the legacy participant room.
Trace and fix the initial render/hydration/hooks issue. A valid invitation URL must open directly without requiring reload.
Test:
Owner creates fresh room
Owner invites Buyer
Buyer opens invitation in clean/incognito session
No React error
Current workspace architecture renders immediately
Buyer sees Buyer-scoped information/actions
Buyer uploads assigned LOI successfully
Owner sees that upload without refresh/reconciliation problems
Owner can also upload a Buyer-responsible LOI and provenance correctly identifies Owner/Deal Coordinator as uploader
Do not modify transaction requirements, extraction, readiness calculations, settlement, sealing, canonicalization, or workflow-pack behavior as part of this fix unless directly required to correct these two defects.
