The current implementation is correct, but Summary should not simply display every workflow_required field.

Summary should be a curated operational snapshot of the transaction.

Rules for Summary:

Display each canonical concept only once. For example, Purchase Price should appear once in Summary even if it is surfaced in multiple Full Record sections.
Prioritize approximately 15–25 fields that tell a user what the transaction is, who is involved, its economics, major diligence status, and readiness to proceed.
Always surface unresolved exceptions even if they are not normally part of Summary: conflicts, changed-source values, rejected confirmations, overdue requests, missing critical information, and blocked approvals.
Workflow required does not automatically mean “show in Summary.” All workflow-required fields remain visible in Full Record.
Keep the existing seven sections, but hide a section from Summary if it has nothing materially useful to show.
Preserve all current Upload doc / Request / Enter / N/A, source guidance, provenance, confirmation history, dependency logic, and Full Record behavior.
For the current Marriott Hotel Acquisition example, Summary should roughly contain:
Transaction: transaction type, current stage, target closing date, jurisdiction, purchase price, earnest money deposit.

Asset / Company: property name, property address, property type, current owner.

Parties: buyer, seller, legal counsel, title/escrow company.

Ownership: proposed acquiring entity, material liens/encumbrances.

Financial: NOI plus any material financing information once applicable. Do not repeat purchase price.

Legal: purchase agreement status, title status, environmental status, material litigation, material lease/lien encumbrances.

Approvals: only material approvals that are actually applicable to this transaction.

Full Record remains the authoritative complete transaction schema.
